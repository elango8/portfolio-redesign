Table Booking Dashboard — Case Study

🔍 Problem

Restaurant owners struggled with manual table reservation processes, leading to overbooking, miscommunication, and poor customer satisfaction. Customers wanted an intuitive way to browse, reserve, and manage their dining experiences.

💡 Solution

Designed a comprehensive dashboard that streamlines the entire table booking flow — from real-time availability checks to automated confirmations — providing both restaurant staff and customers with a seamless experience.

🛠 Technologies

Figma UI/UX Design Prototyping User Research Wireframing

Step 1 — User Research & Discovery
Interviews · Surveys · Competitive Analysis
Step 2 — Information Architecture
User Flows · Sitemaps · Card Sorting
Step 3 — Visual Design & Prototyping
High-Fi Mockups · Interactive Prototypes · Design System

The redesigned dashboard reduced booking time by 60%, improved customer satisfaction scores by 40%, and enabled restaurants to manage 3× more reservations with the same staff.
